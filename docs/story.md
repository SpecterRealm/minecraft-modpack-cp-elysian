## Place in the Veil reveal

Elysian teaches **how to use** the Veil — the *practice*: project mystery and power through vocabulary, ritual, and aesthetics, and be treated with deference. It does **not** say what is underneath the practice or why it works; that is Liminal's. Full ladder: [The Veil — Reveal Ladder](https://github.com/SpecterRealm/minecraft-modpack-cp-liminal/blob/main/docs/series/story.md#the-veil--reveal-ladder).

- Present magic on its own terms. Never say "this is really technology."
- CASPAR frames the technique as operational training, not history.
- The single yellow slip that fits best: a note that the exercise is drawing on doctrine "not in the original curriculum."

## Opening narrative *(draft)*

You stand on almost nothing.

There is no ground here worth the name — a scrap of foothold and a great deal of dark. CASPAR is quick to say this is deliberate. The second module is not about what you can dig up. It is about what you can *call*.

Velara did not always speak plainly about what it knew. Some things were taught the way a craft is taught: gesture, word, ritual, the right thing said in the right way until the world answered. CASPAR does not tell you why the world answers. It tells you how to ask.

Learn to shape what you cannot craft.
